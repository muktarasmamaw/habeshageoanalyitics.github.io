Who We Are

Habesha GeoAnalytics is a geospatial consulting, training, and research platform dedicated to providing high-quality Geographic Information System (GIS), Remote Sensing, Surveying, and Spatial Data Analytics solutions.

We support students, researchers, government institutions, NGOs, and private organizations through spatial analysis, mapping, satellite image processing, geospatial database development, and professional training.

Our mission is to transform geographic data into actionable information for decision-making, sustainable development, environmental monitoring, and infrastructure planning.
