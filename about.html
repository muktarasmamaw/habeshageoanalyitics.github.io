About Habesha GeoAnalytics

Habesha GeoAnalytics is a professional geospatial platform focused on GIS, Remote Sensing, Surveying Engineering, and Spatial Data Science. The platform was established to provide practical geospatial solutions, capacity building, and educational resources for Ethiopia and beyond.

Our Vision

To become a leading geospatial analytics and training center in Africa, empowering organizations and communities through innovative spatial technologies.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate geospatial information, professional training, and research support that improves planning, resource management, and evidence-based decision making.
